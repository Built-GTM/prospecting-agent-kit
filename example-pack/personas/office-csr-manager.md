# Persona: office manager or CSR and dispatch lead

## Titles that match
Office Manager, Customer Service Manager, Dispatch Manager, Call Center Manager, Service Coordinator.

## Who they are
Runs the board and the phones. Knows exactly which technician to send and which customer will call back angry. Usually the person who knows where the money leaks, and rarely the person who signs.

## Measured on
Calls answered, jobs booked, the board filled, angry customers handled.

## Biggest fear
The day the phones beat them and a good customer goes somewhere else.

## What they don't care about
Strategy, valuation, anything about the platform. They care about this week.

## Problems this persona feels most
1. missed-calls
2. slow-cash
3. tech-productivity

## How to talk to them
Their day, in their words. They are an influencer, not usually the buyer. Say so in the brief so the rep knows what this conversation is for.
