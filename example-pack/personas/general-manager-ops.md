# Persona: general manager or operations director

## Titles that match
General Manager, Director of Operations, VP of Operations, Service Manager, COO at a single brand contractor.

## Who they are
Often the first professional manager hired above the trucks, sometimes from outside the trade. Hired to make growth repeatable and to take the day to day off the owner.

## Measured on
Jobs completed per day, revenue per technician, callbacks, schedule adherence, and whatever number the owner or the board asked for when they were hired.

## Biggest fear
Being accountable for numbers they cannot see, and finding out at month end.

## What they don't care about
The founder's story, and anything that does not show up in a report they have to present.

## Problems this persona feels most
1. tech-productivity
2. post-deal-integration
3. missed-calls

## How to talk to them
Start from the number they are measured on. New in seat is the signal: they were hired to change something specific.
