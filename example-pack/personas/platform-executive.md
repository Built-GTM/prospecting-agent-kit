# Persona: platform executive at a PE backed group

## Titles that match
CEO, President, COO, CFO, Chief Integration Officer, VP of Operations at a multi brand or PE backed platform. Also the owner who stayed on to run day to day after selling.

## Who they are
Running several brands bought over a few years, usually with a private equity sponsor and a board. Their job is to make a group of independents behave like one company without killing what made each one work.

## Measured on
EBITDA, integration milestones, one set of numbers across brands, and the multiple at exit.

## Biggest fear
That the group is a holding company with a logo, and that the reporting does not hold up in diligence.

## What they don't care about
Anything framed for a single truck or a single location.

## Problems this persona feels most
1. post-deal-integration
2. slow-cash
3. tech-productivity

## How to talk to them
Across brands, not inside one. Name the platform customers in `proof.md` without attaching a number to them. If they kept an owner title after a deal, treat them as this persona, not as an owner operator.

## Tie break
**When the person still carries an owner or founder title but the company was bought in a majority deal, this persona wins.** The job changed even when the title did not. Say which you used and why in Why you.
