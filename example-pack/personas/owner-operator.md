# Persona: owner operator

## Titles that match
Owner, President, Founder, Owner and President, CEO at a single brand contractor.

## Who they are
Usually came up through the trade. Started with a truck, has 10 to 100 now. Still knows most customers by name and most technicians by their first job. Signs off on anything that costs money.

## Measured on
Revenue and what is left of it. Whether the season was good. Whether they can take a week off.

## Biggest fear
That the business only works because they are in it, and that growth is making the office worse, not better.

## What they don't care about
Feature lists, integrations for their own sake, anything that sounds like enterprise software.

## Problems this persona feels most
1. tech-productivity
2. missed-calls
3. slow-cash

## How to talk to them
Numbers from a contractor their size in their trade. Time back, not features. Never lecture them about their trade.
