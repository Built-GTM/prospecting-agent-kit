# Company: ServiceTitan

## One line
The software the residential and commercial trades run on: CRM, dispatch, scheduling, estimates, invoicing, payments, marketing, and reporting in one system. (found: https://www.servicetitan.com/)

## What we sell
- Product: an AI assisted operating platform for trades contractors, covering dispatch and scheduling, CRM, mobile estimates, project management, service agreements, payroll, payments, marketing, contact center, and reporting. (found: https://www.servicetitan.com/)
- Product family: ServiceTitan, plus FieldRoutes (pest and lawn), Aspire (landscaping), Convex and Conduit for commercial. (found: https://www.servicetitan.com/)
- Trades served: HVAC and mechanical, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage door, chimney, water treatment, refrigeration, fire and life safety, landscaping, pest control, construction and remodeling, kitchen equipment service. (found: https://www.servicetitan.com/)
- Pricing model: per technician. "Our per-technician pricing is designed to fit your business and goals, at any size." Three packages: Starter, Essentials, The Works. (found: https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing)
- Who uses it day to day: technicians in the field, dispatchers, CSRs, office and billing staff, the GM. (inferred from the modules)
- Who buys it: the owner or GM at an independent contractor; a platform executive or CFO at a PE backed multi brand group. (inferred; platform buying is supported by the named platform customers below)
- Scale claim: "over 100,000 contractors". (found: https://www.servicetitan.com/pricing)
- Public company, NYSE. (found: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ServiceTitan)
