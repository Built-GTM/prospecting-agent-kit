# Problem: money earned but not collected

## What it looks like inside the business
The job is done and the invoice is not. Or it is, and it sits. Payment means a check in the mail or a card read over the phone. Somebody in the office chases it, and the ones they forget to chase never get paid.

## Who owns it
The owner. The office manager or an AR clerk day to day. A CFO at a platform.

## What it costs
Cost formula: monthly revenue x the share invoiced late x days late, plus the write offs nobody counts.

## Why they haven't changed
Cash comes in eventually, so it reads as a timing annoyance rather than a leak. The write offs are never added up. (inferred)

## What they say
"We get paid, it just takes a while." "That's the trade."

## Signals that point here
No online payment on the site, hiring an AR or billing clerk, an acquisition that doubled the invoice count, no membership program.

## What good looks like
Payment taken at the truck, invoices out the same day, and a number for what is outstanding.
