# Problem: two companies, two systems, one set of numbers due to the board

## What it looks like inside the business
A platform buys them, or they buy an add on. Now there are two dispatch boards, two price books, two invoice formats, and two versions of what a customer is worth. The board wants one number by the end of the quarter and nobody can produce it without a spreadsheet and a weekend.

## Who owns it
The platform executive, or the owner who stayed on to run day to day after the deal.

## What it costs
Cost formula: locations or brands x the hours per month spent reconciling, plus the price leakage where two price books disagree.
Fill it with sourced numbers only. Brand and location counts are usually public. Ask for the rest.

## Why they haven't changed
Right after a deal everyone is told not to break what works, so both systems stay. The cost shows up one quarter later as reporting nobody trusts. (inferred)

## What they say
"We're leaving them on their system for now." "We'll integrate after the season."

## Signals that point here
An acquisition inside 180 days, a new brand added to the site, a platform name in the press release, a new CFO or integration lead.

## What good looks like
One operating record across brands, one price book, and a number the board can read without a spreadsheet.
