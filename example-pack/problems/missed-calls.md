# Problem: calls that never become booked jobs

## What it looks like inside the business
The phone rings while the CSR is on another call, or after five, or on a Saturday. It goes to voicemail, the customer calls the next contractor, and nobody at the company ever knows the job existed. The site has a phone number and no way to book.

## Who owns it
Office and CSR manager first, the owner right behind them, because it is revenue.

## What it costs
Cost formula: missed calls per week x the share that would have booked x average ticket x 52.
Fill it with sourced numbers only. With no number you can stand behind, say so and ask for it.

## Why they haven't changed
The phone has always rung in the office, and the calls that never came through are invisible. There is no report for a job you never heard about. (inferred)

## What they say
"We're slammed." "We need another CSR." "Summer is just like this."

## Signals that point here
Hiring a CSR or dispatcher, reviews about nobody calling back, no online booking on the site, a high call volume banner.

## What good looks like
Every call answered or booked online, and a number for how many jobs came in after hours.
